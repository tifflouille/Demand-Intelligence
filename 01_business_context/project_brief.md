# Project Brief

## Context

Olist is a Brazilian online marketplace connecting customers and sellers. This project uses historical Olist ecommerce transaction data to explore how a business could better understand and plan for changing customer demand.

The analysis uses ecommerce demand as a general business context.

## Business Value

Demand intelligence can help businesses anticipate changes in purchasing behaviour and make better commercial decisions across:

* Merchandising and product planning
* Sales planning
* Marketing and campaign planning
* Inventory and supply-chain planning
* Commercial prioritisation

## Potential Business Decisions

The analysis could help a business decide:

* Which categories or products deserve greater commercial attention
* Where demand appears stable, growing or volatile
* Where additional planning caution may be needed
* How much confidence to place in different demand forecasts
* Where further data would be needed before making inventory or commercial decisions

The underlying framework could also be applied to other ecommerce models, including DTC businesses.

## Demand Definition

In this project, **demand refers to observed customer purchasing demand**, primarily measured through the number of items sold over time.

Orders and revenue are used as complementary indicators.

Observed sales should not be treated as a perfect measure of true underlying demand, since the dataset does not reliably capture factors such as stockouts or unmet demand.

## Scope

The project focuses on **historical ecommerce demand at monthly category and product level**, with four areas of analysis:

1. Demand evolution
2. Demand volatility
3. Demand forecasting
4. Commercial implications of forecast uncertainty

The project does not attempt to build a complete inventory optimisation or pricing strategy.
